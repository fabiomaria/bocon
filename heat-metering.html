Response time, with its conditions

The featured fast-response sensor specifies t₀.₅ ≤ 2.0 s at ambient temperature, with a 25 °C temperature step and water flow velocity of 0.8 m/s.

Keep those test conditions alongside the figure when assessing suitability for your system.

Temperature range is a selection

The documented ranges are 0–105 °C / 0–150 °C. The brochure lists cable and element options separately, so confirm the combination needed for your application.

Selection essentials

What to bring to
the conversation.

  1. Your system

    Heating or refrigeration, operating temperatures, and minimum temperature difference.

  2. Your installation

    Thread, available insertion space, pressure, and a drawing of the mounting arrangement.

  3. Your meter interface

    Required Pt500 or Pt1000 element and compatibility with the two-wire connection.

  4. Your cable & quantities

    Cable material, routing, required length, and sample or production quantities in sensor pairs.

  5. Your qualification needs

    Relevant certificate scope, approved drawings, and evaluation requirements.
