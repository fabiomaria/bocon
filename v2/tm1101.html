Wire & Probe RTDs TM1101

Flying-lead stainless steel probe RTD

General-purpose 304 or 316L stainless steel protection tube RTD (OD Ø4, 5, 5.2, 6 mm) with direct flying-lead cable exit. Built in single (2-wire, 3-wire, 4-wire) and dual (4-wire, 6-wire) Pt100, Pt500, and Pt1000 configurations for machinery, HVAC manifolds, and test equipment from -50 °C to +350 °C.

Technical specifications

Unified Sensor Datasheet · TM1101 Series
Applications Industrial machinery, HVAC & heat-pump dry wells, bearing housings, and test stands. For the 2-wire EN 1434 matched heat-meter version, see TM1101-5-1500 & TM1101/51-5-1500.
Sensing Element & Accuracy Pt100, Pt500, Pt1000 · Compliant with IEC 60751 (α = 0.00385 °C⁻¹) Tolerance classes: Class B, Class A, Class AA
Measuring Range -50 to +350 °C Dependent on selected lead wire insulation material
Wetted / Housing Material Stainless steel 304 or 316L
Dimensions & Geometry Outer diameter (d): Ø4 mm, Ø5 mm, Ø5.2 mm, Ø6 mm
Standard tube lengths (L): 30 mm, 35 mm, 45 mm, 50 mm (custom lengths to order)
Process Connection Plain cylindrical tube for insertion into dry wells, pockets, or compression fittings
Lead Wire & Termination Single RTD: 2-wire, 3-wire, 4-wire · Dual RTD: 4-wire, 6-wire
Standard lead length (G): 1500, 2000, 2500, 3000 mm Insulation options: PVC (-30…+105 °C) · Silicone (-50…+200 °C) · PTFE (-50…+250 °C) · Fibreglass (-50…+350 °C)
Performance & Electrical Recommended excitation current: ≤ 0.5 mA (Pt100) · ≤ 0.2 mA (Pt500) · ≤ 0.1 mA (Pt1000) Insulation resistance: R ≥ 100 MΩ at room temperature (25 °C, ≤ 50% RH)
Protection & Ratings IP65 Standard IP68 Optional ISO 9001

Wiring configurations

Available in single-element 2/3/4-wire and dual-element 4/6-wire lead terminations.
