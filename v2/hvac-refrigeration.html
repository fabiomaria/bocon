RTD probes for air ducts, chilled-water headers, and pipe surfaces

Air handling units, heat pumps, and industrial refrigeration skids measure temperature across three physical interfaces: moving air inside ducts, pressurized water or glycol inside manifolds, and outer pipe walls where drilling a port is impractical. We build dedicated Pt100, Pt500, and Pt1000 constructions for each point.

Preventing condensation ingress and lead-wire offset

Chilled-water and refrigeration pipes operate below ambient dew point, causing continuous condensation or frost cycling around probe stems and cable exits. Addressing moisture sealing, surface contact, and lead resistance during specification prevents field drift.

IP68 transition sealing on cold lines
Standard flying-lead probes are sealed to IP65. Where evaporator wells or chilled-water manifolds sweat continuously, specify the optional IP68 seal on TM1101 with silicone or PTFE cable insulation to maintain ≥ 100 MΩ insulation resistance.
Thermal coupling on clamp-on pipe sensors
When strapping TM1111-1, TM1111-2, or TM1111-3 onto existing pipework, apply thermal paste under the curved contact saddle and wrap closed-cell pipe insulation over the sensor head so room air does not bias the surface reading.
Pt1000 vs. 3-wire Pt100 on long BMS runs
In a 2-wire Pt100 circuit, 1 Ω of cable resistance adds roughly 2.6 °C of error. Using Pt1000 reduces lead error tenfold (~0.26 °C/Ω), while 3-wire or 4-wire Pt100 compensates lead resistance at the controller.

Selection Guide

Choosing the right probe for each HVAC zone

  1. Supply, return, and fresh air ducts (TM1705)

    Use the flanged TM1705 (Ø14 × 250 mm stem with a 35 mm slotted tip) in sheet-metal air ducts and AHU plenums. The sliding flange adjusts insertion depth so the vented tip sits in the active airstream.

  2. Retrofit hydronic pipes & manifolds (TM1111-1 / -2 / -3)

    Use TM1111-1 or TM1111-2 where a rugged 304 stainless steel contact block is needed, or choose the copper-saddle TM1111-3 (25 × 20 × 1 mm copper plate) for faster surface heat transfer on copper or steel pipes.

  3. Heat pump skids & evaporator pockets (TM1101)

    Use the flying-lead TM1101 inside brazed dry wells or compression fittings. Order pre-cut harness lengths with PVC, silicone, PTFE, or fibreglass leads.

  4. Plant-room headers & cooling towers (TM1401)

    Use the cast-aluminium terminal head TM1401 on main chilled-water and condenser-water headers where field conduit cable terminates at a ceramic terminal block or head-mounted 4–20 mA transmitter.
