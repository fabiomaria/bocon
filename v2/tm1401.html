Junction Box RTDs TM1401 · TM1401-8-85-1500 · TM1401/85

J-type cast-aluminium terminal head RTD

Industrial platinum resistance thermometer (-50 °C to +400 °C) combining a compact cast-aluminium J-type junction box (57 × 63 mm, IP65) with a 304 or 316L stainless steel protection tube (OD Ø4 to Ø11 mm, insertion lengths from 35 mm to 2000 mm).

Technical specifications

Unified Sensor Datasheet · TM1401 Series
Applications Process vessels, plant-room headers, boilers, chillers, and district heating mains. For EN 1434 matched heat-meter pairs built on this platform (TM1401-8-85-1500 and TM1401/85), see Heat Meter Sensors.
Sensing Element & Accuracy Pt100, Pt500, Pt1000 · Compliant with IEC 60751 (α = 0.00385 °C⁻¹) Tolerance classes: Class B, Class A, Class AA (or EN 1434 matched pairs on TM1401-8-85-1500 / TM1401/85)
Measuring Range -50 to +400 °C Heat meter variants: 0 to 105 / 150 °C (TM1401-8-85-1500) · 0 to 180 °C (TM1401/85)
Wetted / Housing Material Protection tube: Stainless steel 304 or 316L
Terminal head: Cast aluminium (J-type enclosure, 57 × 63 mm)
Dimensions & Geometry Outer diameter (D): Ø4, Ø5, Ø6, Ø8, Ø9, Ø11 mm
Standard insertion lengths (L): 35, 65, 90, 125, 150, 200, 250, 300, 400, 500, 750, 1000, 1500, 2000 mm
Process Connection Plain stem (TM1401), fixed thread (TM1402), union nut (TM1403), flange (TM1407), or G1/2 pocket (TM1401/85)
Lead Wire & Termination Ceramic terminal block inside J-type head
Single RTD: 2-wire, 3-wire, 4-wire · Dual RTD: 4-wire
Performance & Electrical Recommended excitation current: ≤ 0.5 mA (Pt100) · ≤ 0.2 mA (Pt500) · ≤ 0.1 mA (Pt1000) Insulation resistance: R ≥ 100 MΩ at room temperature (25 °C, ≤ 50% RH)
Protection & Ratings IP65 ISO 9001

Terminal block wiring

Internal ceramic terminal block layouts for single 2/3/4-wire and dual 4-wire elements.
