Application · Thermal Energy Metering

Matched temperature sensor pairs for heating and cooling meters

Thermal energy meters calculate heat transfer from water volume and the temperature difference (ΔT) between supply and return lines. We calibrate and pair Pt100, Pt500, and Pt1000 sensors to EN 1434-2015 and GB/T 32224-2020 so sensor offset does not skew billing at low ΔT.

Why flow and return sensors are paired at the factory

Two uncalibrated Class B sensors can sit at opposite ends of their tolerance band, creating a differential offset that exceeds the entire ΔT of a chilled-water or low-temperature heating loop. We bath-test each element at three temperatures, compute its Callendar–Van Dusen constants, and pair sensors whose resistance curves track each other across the operating span.

Direct immersion vs. thermowell mounting
Compact DN15–DN40 residential meters typically use M10 × 1 direct-immersion probes (TM1101-5-1500 or stepped TM1101/51-5-1500), while larger district heating pipes use G1/2 long-tube or terminal-head assemblies (TM1401-8-85-1500 or TM1401/85).
Symmetrical 2-wire lead resistance
On 2-wire pairs, both sensors are supplied with equal cable lengths (Ø4.2 mm PVC, PU, or silicone jacket) so lead resistance cancels out in the differential measurement.
