Selecting a heat meter pair

  1. Heating vs. cooling duty

    Cooling loops operate at small temperature differences and use ±0.06 °C matching accuracy down to ΔTmin = 2 K; heating loops use ±0.1 °C down to ΔTmin = 3 K.

  2. Process connection & tube geometry

    Select M10 × 1 direct-immersion probes (TM1101 series) for compact DN15–DN40 meter bodies and ball valves, or G1/2 long-tube and junction-head models (TM1401 series) for larger sub-station piping.

  3. Calculator input & wiring

    Match Pt500 or Pt1000 (2-wire) to your ultrasonic or mechanical calculator, or choose TM1401/85 where Pt100 3-wire termination is required.

  4. Response speed

    Use the stepped-tube TM1101/51-5-1500 (Ø3.7 × 10 mm reduced tip) where t₀.₅ ≤ 2.0 s step response is required.
