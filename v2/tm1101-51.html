Heat Meter Sensors TM1101-5-1500 · TM1101/51-5-1500

Direct-immersion temperature sensor pairs for heat meters

Matched Pt500 and Pt1000 temperature sensor pairs for building heating and cooling energy meters. Built in a straight Ø5 × 45 mm tube (TM1101-5-1500) and a fast-response stepped Ø3.7 × 10 – Ø5 × 45 mm tube (TM1101/51-5-1500) with M10 × 1 brass mounting threads.

Technical specifications

Unified Sensor Datasheet · TM1101 Heat Meter Series
Applications Building thermal energy metering (heating and cooling systems). For single unpaired industrial probes, see TM1101 Wire-Type RTD.
Sensing Element & Accuracy Pt500, Pt1000 (optional) · Compliant with IEC 751 Pair matching accuracy: ±0.06 °C (refrigeration, ΔTmin 2 K) · ±0.1 °C (heating, ΔTmin 3 K)
Measuring Range 0 to 105 °C / 0 to 150 °C
Wetted / Housing Material Stainless steel SUS316L
Dimensions & Geometry TM1101/51-5-1500: Stepped tube Ø3.7 × 10 mm – Ø5 × 45 mm
TM1101-5-1500: Straight tube Ø5 × 45 mm
Process Connection M10 × 1 thread
Lead Wire & Termination 2-wire connecting cable, Ø4.2 mm TM1101/51-5-1500: PVC / PU / Silicone cable · TM1101-5-1500: PVC / Silicone cable
Performance & Electrical TM1101/51-5-1500: t₀.₅ ≤ 2.0 s · TM1101-5-1500: t₀.₅ ≤ 3.0 s Measured at ambient temperature, 25 °C step, water flow velocity 0.8 m/s Insulation resistance: R ≥ 100 MΩ at 100 V DC (room temperature)
Protection & Ratings IP68 PN25 MID Approved Standards: GB/T 32224-2020 and EN 1434-2015

Wiring configuration

Standard 2-wire connection with white and red leads connected across the platinum sensing element.
