Hygienic, HVAC & Ex RTDs TM1705

Flanged air-duct RTD temperature sensor

Dedicated HVAC air-duct sensor (-50 °C to +180 °C) combining a sealed ABS enclosure (91.5 × 66.5 mm, IP65), an adjustable sliding duct flange, and a Ø14 × 250 mm tube with a 35 mm slotted tip for fast air-flow response.

Technical specifications

Unified Sensor Datasheet · TM1705 Series
Applications Supply, return, and fresh air ventilation ducts, air handling units (AHUs), cleanroom plenums, and building automation systems
Sensing Element & Accuracy Pt100, Pt1000 · Compliant with IEC 60751 (α = 0.00385 °C⁻¹) Tolerance classes: Class B, Class A
Measuring Range -50 to +180 °C
Wetted / Housing Material ABS junction box (91.5 × 66.5 × 27 mm) with sliding mounting flange and slotted protection stem
Dimensions & Geometry Protection stem: Ø14 × 250 mm (with Ø14 × 35 mm slotted air-flow tip)
Mounting flange: 56.6 mm width · 50 mm bolt pitch · 3 × Ø5 mm mounting holes
Process Connection Adjustable sliding duct flange (3 × Ø5 mm screw holes on 50 mm pitch)
Lead Wire & Termination Single 2-wire, 3-wire flying lead · Standard lead lengths (G): 1500, 2000, 2500, 3000 mm Insulation options: PVC (-30…+105 °C) · Silicone (-50…+200 °C) · PTFE (-50…+250 °C) · Fibreglass (-50…+350 °C)
Performance & Electrical Recommended excitation current: ≤ 0.5 mA (Pt100) · ≤ 0.1 mA (Pt1000) Insulation resistance: R ≥ 100 MΩ at room temperature (25 °C, ≤ 50% RH)
Protection & Ratings IP65 ISO 9001
