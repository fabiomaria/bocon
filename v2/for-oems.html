2. Samples, testing, and qualification

Before locking a sensor part number for series production, OEM engineering teams verify mechanical fit, thermal response time, insulation resistance, and calibration stability. We manufacture both the temperature sensors and the liquid/wind-tunnel thermal response test equipment used to verify them.

Three-point bath calibration & EN 1434 pairing
Heat-meter pairs are bath-tested across reference temperatures to compute individual Callendar–Van Dusen coefficients (R₀, A, B) and verify pairing error within ±0.06 °C (cooling, ΔTmin 2 K) or ±0.1 °C (heating, ΔTmin 3 K).
In-house thermal response time testing (t₀.₅ / t₀.₉)
We measure step response time in regulated water flow (0.8 m/s or 0.4 m/s per EN 1434 / GB/T 32224) and airflow channels using Bocon’s own thermal response testing apparatus.
Insulation resistance & pressure verification
Every production batch is checked for room-temperature insulation resistance (R ≥ 100 MΩ at 100 V DC) and rated enclosure sealing (IP65 or IP68 submersible transition).
Quality management & regulatory approvals
Manufactured under ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and ISO 45001 quality systems, with MID approval on designated heat-meter sensor series (TM1101-5-1500 and TM1101/51-5-1500).

3. Supply and technical support

  1. Locked drawing and part code

    Once your engineering team approves the prototype samples, we assign a dedicated part code and lock the bill of materials, cable specification, and calibration fixture settings for repeat orders.

  2. Serialised pair tagging

    Matched heat-meter pairs are marked with matching serial numbers and flow/return colour bands so assembly-line technicians never mix sensors from different calibration bins.

  3. Direct engineering contact

    Specification reviews, tolerance questions, and drawing revisions are handled directly by our sensor engineering team.

  4. Line-ready export packaging

    Probes are packed in partitioned trays or paired bags tailored to your incoming inspection and meter assembly workflow.

What to include when requesting OEM samples

  • Target temperature range and medium (water, air, glycol, oil)
  • Sensing element (Pt100, Pt500, Pt1000), tolerance class, and wiring (2W, 3W, 4W, dual, or matched pair)
  • Sheath outer diameter, insertion length, and process thread or drawing
  • Cable insulation material, lead length, and termination style
  • Sample quantity for qualification and estimated annual batch volume
